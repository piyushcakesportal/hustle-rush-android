Hustle Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Hustle Rush is a mobile game published by Cakesportal. No account is required to play.

Information stored on your device

The game stores scores, unlocked levels, earned stars, and sound and vibration preferences locally on your device. This information provides game functionality and is not sent to the developer.

Advertising

Hustle Rush uses Google AdMob to display rewarded and interstitial advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, advertising interactions, and other technical information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Processing depends on your device settings, region, and consent choices.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at Google's partner-sites policy and review Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app presents Google's consent message before requesting ads. A privacy-options control is displayed inside the app when Google indicates that it is required, allowing you to review available choices.

Permissions

The app uses internet and network-state access to load advertisements and vibration access for optional haptic feedback. It does not request access to location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Children

Hustle Rush is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from users.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the email address displayed on the Hustle Rush Google Play listing.
